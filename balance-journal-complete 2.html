About this journal

Balance is a rules-based journal for gluten-free eating with thoughtful attention to protein, iron-rich foods, and overall balance. It never counts calories or macros. It simply remembers what you’ve logged over days, notices soft patterns, and offers quiet, naturally gluten-free ideas so you can stay nourished without the arithmetic.
